# Generated material atlases

Both atlases were generated with the built-in image_gen tool and copied into this project. Each image is a 2×2 atlas; graphics.mjs samples its quadrants into local 512×512 textures. No original Counter-Strike assets are included.

- dust-material-atlas.png: sandstone, pavement, crate timber, weathered plaster.
- soldier-material-atlas.png: camouflage, military canvas, glove leather, facial albedo.

## Environment prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: single square 2x2 game environment texture atlas for a 3D classic tactical shooter inspired by Counter-Strike 1.6 de_dust. Generate one 2048x2048 square image consisting of exactly four equal square material swatches, absolutely no spacing, borders or labels. TOP LEFT: weathered warm ochre sandstone block wall, horizontally coursed medium irregular rectangular stone blocks, recessed mortar, realistic chipped edges, dirt and porous surface; TOP RIGHT: dusty pale beige stone pavement with worn square flagstones, sand in thin joints, restrained detail; BOTTOM LEFT: old dark brown wooden shipping crate front, vertical timber planks, diagonal wooden reinforcement, iron nails, grime; BOTTOM RIGHT: aged warm beige plaster wall, peeling plaster with small exposed stone patches and hairline cracks, subtle water stains. Each quadrant a flat orthographic material surface filling its entire quadrant, no perspective, no surrounding scene, no cast shadows, diffuse albedo only. Strong believable texture detail reminiscent of authored late 1990s/early 2000s GoldSrc textures, gritty and desaturated, not cartoon, no modern glossy surfaces, no text, no logo, no weapons. All quadrant edges visually compatible with repeating texture tiling.

## Soldier prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: ONE square 2x2 character material texture atlas for detailed low-poly 3D human soldiers in a Counter-Strike 1.6 inspired tactical FPS. Four equal square quadrants meeting at exact image center, NO borders, no spacing, no labels. TOP LEFT: muted tan and olive desert camouflage cotton uniform fabric, small camouflage shapes with weave, seams-free repeating material. TOP RIGHT: worn dark olive military canvas, tightly woven fabric, subtle stitched horizontal bands, scuffs, no perspective. BOTTOM LEFT: black and charcoal tactical glove leather material with subtle creases, matte, dusty, flat surface. BOTTOM RIGHT: flat front projection facial texture of a realistic adult male soldier, neutral expression, cropped to fill quadrant from forehead to chin, no hair, no shoulders, no background, symmetrical frontal eyes and nose, short stubble, natural skin and detail, diffuse evenly lit. Intended as albedo maps applied to polygon characters, classic 2000s realistic game art with sharp authored detail, not cartoon or blocky. No shadows, no lighting gradients, no text, no logos. Other three material quadrants are flat orthographic continuous texture surfaces, no garments silhouettes, no objects.
